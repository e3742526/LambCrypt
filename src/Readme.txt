LambCrypt
=========

LambCrypt is an independently maintained disk-encryption fork of VeraCrypt.
Based on TrueCrypt, freely available at http://www.truecrypt.org/

See ../README.md for build instructions and ../UPSTREAM.md for attribution.
In a standalone archive, the current build instructions and source are at
https://github.com/e3742526/LambCrypt#readme

The license terms are in License.txt. Copyright and third-party notices
are in NOTICE and in the source files.

Report LambCrypt issues at https://github.com/e3742526/LambCrypt/issues
